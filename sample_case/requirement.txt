Acme Corp - Analytics Rollout: Requirement Document (Draft v1)
Prepared by: Sarah Kim, VP Operations

1. Sales Performance Dashboard
   - Revenue and profit margin by product, product category, region, and customer segment.
   - Reporting period: calendar year 2025.
   - Profit margin = revenue minus standard product cost.

2. Order Cancellation Visibility
   - Ability to report on the reason each cancelled order was cancelled.

3. Support SLA Tracking
   - Ticket resolution time measured from creation to first Resolved/Closed status change.
   - Flag tickets that breach SLA thresholds by priority.

4. Invoice Aging
   - Flag invoices more than 30 days past their due date as past due.
   - Nice-to-have: breakdown of paid invoices by payment method.

5. Product Catalog Health
   - Nice-to-have: count of active vs. inactive products.

Out of scope for this phase: any data outside the current CRM/ERP system, and any predictive/
forecasting analytics.
